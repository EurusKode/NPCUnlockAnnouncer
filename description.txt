NPC Unlock Announcer
--------------------
Never miss a new villager again!
This mod adds a premium, RPG-style announcement banner whenever a Town NPC (or town pet) arrives in your world.

Features:
- Visual Themes: Classic (black & gold), Modern (animated neon glassmorphism), Crimson, Wood, and Jungle.
- Audio Presets: Choose from multiple built-in sounds (Magic Ding, Coins, Max Mana, and theme-specific sounds) with adjustable volume and banner duration.
- Custom Subtitles: Witty, unique descriptions for every vanilla NPC.
- Mod Compatibility: Fully compatible with Calamity, Thorium, AlchemistNPC, Magic Storage, Fargo's, and integrates with Census.

Languages:
- English
- Español 
*Note: Other languages (DE, IT, FR, RU, ZH-CN, PT-BR, PL) are available but have not went through revision.

NPC Unlock Announcer

¡Nunca más te pierdas la llegada de un aldeano!
Este mod añade un elegante banner estilo RPG cada vez que un NPC del pueblo (o mascota) llega a tu mundo.

- Temas: Clásico (negro y dorado), Moderno (neón animado), Carmesí, Madera y Selva.
